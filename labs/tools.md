# Tools — Generative AI for Adobe Photoshop (C16)

**Course Code:** C16  |  **Version v1.0 · 27 July 2026**

This course uses the generative AI built into **Adobe Photoshop** (powered by
**Adobe Firefly**) to edit and create images, plus the **Adobe Firefly web app**
for standalone text-to-image generation. Everything is done inside Photoshop except
where a lab points you to the Firefly web app.

## Accounts and access

- **Adobe Photoshop** installed and updated to a current release with **Generative Fill**, **Generative Expand** and the **Remove** tool — a free trial is available from **adobe.com**. The trainer will confirm the version at the start of the day.
- An **Adobe account** signed in to Photoshop, with **Firefly generative credits** available. Each generation uses credits; the labs stay within a modest number, but check your balance first.
- An **internet connection** — the generative features process in the cloud, so they must be online.
- The **Adobe Firefly web app** (**firefly.adobe.com**), used in Lab 7 for standalone text-to-image generation, is included with your Adobe account.

## What you use in each tool

| Tool / feature | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Contextual Task Bar** | The on-canvas bar that offers Generative Fill and the right next action |
| **Generative Fill** | Adding content from a prompt, and removing content with an empty prompt |
| **Generative Expand** | Extending the canvas so Photoshop generates new image to re-frame or reformat |
| **Remove tool (AI Remove)** | One-stroke cleanup of small distractions and blemishes |
| **Select Subject / Select and Mask** | Isolating the product before replacing its background |
| **Text-to-image (Firefly web app / generate)** | Creating a brand-new lifestyle scene from a text prompt |
| **Reference image & style controls** | Steering a generated image's composition, style, colour and lighting |
| **Neural Filters** | AI portrait edits — Skin Smoothing, Smart Portrait, Colorize and more |
| **Layer masks & blend modes** | Blending generative layers into one seamless composite |
| **Export As / Content Credentials** | Exporting the deliverable and attaching AI-provenance metadata |

## What you produce

- A **clean hero shot** of the Solace serum — content added and removed, blemishes cleaned.
- A **wide banner** via Generative Expand and a **replaced background**.
- A **text-to-image lifestyle scene** and an **AI-retouched portrait**.
- A **finished, layered campaign key visual** exported in three formats and checked for commercial use.

## Safety and responsible use

- Use only images, people and brands you are **authorised** to use; do not upload a real company's product photo, a copyrighted image, or a photo of a person without their **consent**.
- Check Adobe's **current generative-AI usage terms** and your generative-credit limits before you publish, share or sell a result — Firefly is designed for commercial safety, but the images, people and brands you bring in are your responsibility.
- Note that the generative features **process your images in the cloud** under Adobe's terms of service; do not upload confidential material.
- **Disclose** AI use where appropriate, avoid deceptive edits (especially to a person's body or identity), and use **Content Credentials** to record provenance.
