# Solace Radiance Serum — Reference Pack

This pack is the supplied sample product for the **Generative AI for Adobe
Photoshop (C16)** labs. Every lab builds one connected deliverable — a **brand
campaign key visual** for the **Solace Radiance Serum** by the fictional botanical
skincare studio **Aster & Vale** — so you have a clear, consistent brief to work
from. You may substitute your own **non-confidential** product anywhere you prefer;
if you do, follow the same structure below for your own product.

## What's in the pack

| File | Use it in | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| [prompt-sheet.md](prompt-sheet.md) | Labs 2, 3, 6, 7 | The product description and ready-to-adapt prompt building blocks |
| [style-notes.md](style-notes.md) | Labs 2, 6, 8, 9 | The look, colours, lighting and mood the campaign should have |
| [reference-image-guide.md](reference-image-guide.md) | Lab 7 | How to obtain and prepare a reference image for text-to-image |
| [../starter-files/](../starter-files/) | Labs 1, 8 | The two starter photos — the serum product shot and the model portrait |

## The product in one line

The **Solace Radiance Serum** — a small **amber-glass dropper bottle** with a
**matte cream label** and a **brushed-gold cap**, sold as a calm, natural, premium
skincare product; about 12 cm tall.

## Responsible use

Use only images and people you are authorised to use. The Solace serum and Aster &
Vale are a fictional brand created for this course; keep to it (or your own original
product) rather than copying a real, branded product, and only edit a photo of a
person who has consented. See [../tools.md](../tools.md) for the full responsible-use notes.
