# Solace Radiance Serum — Style Notes

Use these notes in **Labs 2, 6, 8 and 9** to keep the campaign's look consistent
from the first prompt through to the final composite.

## Overall feel

Calm, natural and premium — a considered botanical skincare product, not a loud
promo and not a clinical pack shot. Think "quiet luxury wellness brand": warm,
tactile, uncluttered and photogenic, with plenty of soft, breathing space around
the product.

## Materials and colours

| Element | Look | Colour (approx. hex) |
|---|---|---|
| Bottle glass | Amber / honey glass, softly lit | `#A5641E` |
| Label | Matte cream, minimal type | `#F3E9D6` |
| Cap | Brushed gold, soft (not mirror) reflections | `#C6A25A` |
| Backgrounds | Warm cream and soft stone | `#EFE7D8` |
| Botanical accents | Muted sage / eucalyptus green | `#8AA07E` |
| Deep accent | Warm charcoal for contrast and text | `#2B2B2B` |

## Lighting

- **Soft natural daylight from the left**, gently diffused — the same direction in
  every element so the pieces composite together believably.
- Gentle, soft-edged shadows; no harsh, hard highlights on the glass.
- A little warmth overall — golden rather than cool blue.

## Level of detail

- Keep the composition **clean and uncluttered** — the product is the hero.
- Add **just enough** supporting interest: a sprig of eucalyptus, a couple of dried
  botanicals, a soft shadow — never a crowded flat-lay.
- Leave **clear negative space** for a headline (especially in the wide banner).

## What to avoid

- A cold, clinical or over-bright look — Solace is warm and calm.
- Harsh shadows or blown highlights on the glass and cap.
- Clutter, competing props, stray text or any other brand's logo.
- Over-retouched, plastic-looking skin on the model — keep people natural.
