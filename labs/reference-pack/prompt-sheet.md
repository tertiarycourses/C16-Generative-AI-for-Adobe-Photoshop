# Solace Radiance Serum — Prompt Sheet

Use this sheet in **Labs 2, 3, 6 and 7** to build your Generative Fill prompts and
your text-to-image prompt. A strong prompt names the **subject**, adds a few
**details**, sets a **style** and **lighting**, and gives a short **negative** cue
for what to avoid. Describe what you WANT to see in the area — not an instruction.
Fill the slots below (or adapt them to your own product).

## The product

**Solace Radiance Serum** by Aster & Vale — a small amber-glass dropper bottle with
a matte cream label and a brushed-gold cap. One clean, premium object; calm and natural.

## Prompt building blocks (mix and match)

**[SUBJECT]**
> the Solace Radiance Serum bottle — an amber-glass dropper bottle with a cream label and a gold cap.

**[DETAILS]**
> a small eucalyptus sprig and a few dried botanicals resting nearby, a soft contact shadow, subtle natural texture.

**[STYLE]**
> calm, minimalist, premium skincare style; clean and uncluttered.  *(alternatives: lush botanical · warm editorial · bright and airy)*

**[LIGHTING]**
> soft natural daylight from the left, gentle diffused shadows.  *(alternatives: warm golden-hour light · bright studio softbox)*

**[NEGATIVE]**
> no text, no logos, no clutter, no harsh shadows, no extra bottles, no busy background.
