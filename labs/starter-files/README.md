# Solace Starter Files

These are the two starter photos the labs begin from. Your trainer supplies them on
the day; if you are working on your own, create or substitute equivalents following
the notes below. Copy whichever files you use into your **Solace-campaign** project
folder before Lab 1.

| File | Used in | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `solace-serum.jpg` | Labs 1–6 | A plain product shot of the Solace Radiance Serum bottle on a simple cream surface |
| `solace-model.jpg` | Lab 8 | A portrait of a person holding or applying the serum, for AI retouching |

## `solace-serum.jpg` — the product shot

A single amber-glass dropper bottle with a matte cream label and a brushed-gold
cap, standing on a plain, softly-lit cream surface, lit from the left, with room
around it. If you need to create one, generate it in the Firefly web app with a
prompt from the [reference-pack/prompt-sheet.md](../reference-pack/prompt-sheet.md),
or photograph a small dropper bottle of your own on a plain background.

## `solace-model.jpg` — the portrait

A natural, well-lit portrait of a person holding or applying a small serum bottle,
plain or softly blurred background, lit from the left to match the product.

> **Consent and rights:** only use a photo of a person who has **consented** to
> being edited and used in this way. If you do not have one, generate a portrait in
> the Firefly web app (an AI-generated person is not a real, identifiable
> individual) or skip the personal likeness and retouch a non-portrait image
> instead. Never edit a photo of a real person without permission.

## Substituting your own product

You may use your own **non-confidential** product throughout. Replace both starter
files with your own equivalents, keep the same lighting direction across them, and
adapt the reference-pack prompts to your product.
